## What this step is

A simple support process for Via Wash: how requests come in, how they are recorded, and when they move up to a specialist function in IT.

## Why it matters for Via Wash

A clear process means problems are fixed consistently, and a support issue that turns out to be a security issue reaches the right people quickly.

## What will be done

- Choose a simple way to record requests, such as a Microsoft List
- Define support tiers and when to escalate
- Log real requests from the lab work

## How it will be checked

- Requests from the project are recorded and resolved
