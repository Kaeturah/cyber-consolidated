## Why this stage comes last

Investigating an incident properly means answering questions that cut across the whole environment. Who was involved? Which device did they use? What access did they have? Which controls responded, and what evidence was left behind? Every earlier stage provides part of the answer, which is why this one can only come at the end.

## What it depends on

Everything before it: the identities from stage 1, the devices from stage 2, the protection from stage 3, the infrastructure from stage 4 and the detections from stage 5.

## What it will cover

- Incident scenarios and how severity is decided
- An investigation workflow and how evidence is handled
- Containment and recovery actions
- Runbooks and checks that the response works
- Lessons learned and follow-up actions

## How it will be validated

Each simulated incident will be worked from first alert to closure, with the investigation notes, the actions taken and the outcome recorded.

## Why it matters

This is where the series comes full circle. The incidents being investigated happen in an environment that was designed and built here, so every finding can be traced back to a decision made in an earlier chapter.
