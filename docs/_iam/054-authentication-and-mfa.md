## What this step is

Setting up multi-factor authentication and the sign-in methods Via Wash staff will use.

## Why it matters for Via Wash

Passwords alone are guessed, phished and reused. A second factor stops most account takeovers.

## What will be done

- Choose the allowed sign-in methods, such as Microsoft Authenticator
- Turn on registration for MFA
- Plan self-service password reset

## How it will be checked

- A test user is asked for a second factor at sign-in
