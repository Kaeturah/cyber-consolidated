## What this step is

The new starter’s first sign-in: changing the temporary password, registering multi-factor authentication, and checking their access works.

## Why it matters for Via Wash

The real test of onboarding is the employee’s experience. This step checks everything from their side, not just from the admin centre.

## What will be done

- Sign in as the new starter and register MFA
- Open their files, email and apps
- Try to open something they shouldn’t reach, and confirm it is blocked

## How it will be checked

- Everything they need works, and access outside their role is refused
