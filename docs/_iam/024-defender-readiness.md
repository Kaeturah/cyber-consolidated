## What this step is

A check of Microsoft Defender, the family of security services that protects devices, email and identities. It confirms what the E5 licence includes and records the starting state of the Defender portal before any device is onboarded.

## Why it matters for Via Wash

Stage 3 depends on Defender, and its features are easy to assume rather than check. A licence that includes Defender for Endpoint doesn’t mean any device is protected; this step separates what is available from what is actually running.

## What will be done

- Open the Microsoft Defender portal and record what it shows
- Identify which Defender services the licence includes, for endpoints, email and identity
- Record that no devices are onboarded yet
- Note the onboarding options available for Windows devices

## How it will be checked

- The Defender portal opens for the Cyber Consolidated tenant
- The services listed match the E5 licence
- The device inventory confirms the starting state

## Key terms

- **Defender for Endpoint**: protection, detection and response for devices
- **Defender for Office 365**: protection for email and collaboration
- **Onboarding**: connecting a device so Defender can protect and monitor it
