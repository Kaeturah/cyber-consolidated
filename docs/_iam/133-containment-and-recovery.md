## What this step is

Containing a simulated incident, recovering, and writing up lessons learned.

## Why it matters for Via Wash

Response is only complete when the environment is safe again and the cause is fixed, so it doesn’t happen twice.

## What will be done

- Contain: for example disable an account, revoke sessions or isolate a device
- Recover and confirm normal service
- Write a short lessons-learned record

## How it will be checked

- The containment action is recorded and verified
- Follow-up actions are listed
