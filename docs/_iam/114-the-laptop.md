## What this step is

Getting the new starter’s laptop ready: enrolled in Intune, or joined to the domain on the on-premises route.

## Why it matters for Via Wash

A new starter can’t work without a trusted device, and preparing it should not depend on IT setting each machine up by hand.

## What will be done

- Prepare the test client for the new starter
- Enrol it with the new starter’s account
- Confirm it is compliant

## How it will be checked

- The device appears as managed and assigned to the new starter
