## What this step is

Checking that putting the new starter in the Operations group automatically gives them their licence, shared files and app access.

## Why it matters for Via Wash

This is the point of group-based design. If access has to be granted item by item, the design has failed.

## What will be done

- Confirm automatic group membership from the department field
- Check group-based licensing assigned the licence
- Check access to the Operations files and apps

## How it will be checked

- The new starter has exactly the Operations access, and nothing from other departments
