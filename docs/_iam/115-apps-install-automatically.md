## What this step is

Watching the Operations apps install on the new starter’s laptop automatically, through Intune in the cloud route and Group Policy in the on-premises route.

## Why it matters for Via Wash

This is what the user asked to see working: group membership leading straight to a ready-to-use machine.

## What will be done

- Assign the apps to the Operations group in Intune
- Assign the on-premises equivalent through Group Policy
- Sign in and watch the installs

## How it will be checked

- Each assigned app installs without anyone touching the device
