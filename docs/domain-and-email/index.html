What this project is

This site needed somewhere to live before it could document anything. This short project records how that happened: registering the domain, pointing it at a website, giving the business a mailbox, and publishing the DNS records that let other mail servers trust it.

It is also a small, complete example of the approach used across every project here. Each step says what was done, what the screenshots show, and what they don’t prove yet.
