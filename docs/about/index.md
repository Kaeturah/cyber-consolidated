## Hello, I’m Franklin

I’m building my skills across IT support, identity, cloud and security, and I learn best by building real things. This site is where those builds are recorded properly: what I set out to do, why I made each decision, what I actually configured, how I tested it, and what I would do differently.

## Why “consolidated”

Most of what I learn arrives in pieces: a course module here, a lab there, a fix found at two in the morning. Cyber Consolidated brings those pieces together into complete projects, each with a clear story from start to finish.

## How the work is documented

- **Real environments.** Projects run in genuine test environments and are tied to a real business need wherever possible. [Via Wash]({{ '/via-wash/' | relative_url }}) is a business in development, not an invented company.
- **Evidence before claims.** Every chapter carries a status. Something is only marked as documented when evidence supports it.
- **Genuine screenshots only.** Screenshots come straight from the environment and are redacted before publishing. Nothing is mocked up to look finished.
- **Limits stated plainly.** A licence or a visible menu option isn’t a working control, and the pages say so.
- **Private details stay private.** Tenant and subscription IDs, account names, billing and contact details are never published.
